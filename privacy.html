About this policy

This policy explains how PATTAM: Kite Battle handles information. PATTAM is an offline kite-fighting game. The current closed-test configuration has advertising disabled and does not require a player account. This policy also explains the advertising features integrated into the game if they are enabled in a later configuration.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, earned currency, selected kite colours, settings, scores and gameplay-control recordings locally. Recordings support local ghost challenges; they do not record your voice, camera or identity. DAAR GAMES does not operate a gameplay-data server for this version. No real in-app purchase service is integrated.

Advertising and third-party services

The game includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. In the current closed-test configuration, ad serving is disabled and the game's ad flow does not request ads or initialize the advertising SDK.

When advertising is enabled, optional rewarded ads and limited ads between eligible duels may be offered. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact information depends on permissions, configuration and available services. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Children and age eligibility

PATTAM is intended for a mixed audience, including children and older players. When advertising is enabled, the game asks for a birth year through a neutral age screen before entering the advertising flow. Only an eligibility category is stored locally. The entered birth year is cleared and is not stored or sent to DAAR GAMES or Google by this screen.

Children, teens and players who skip the question play without advertising. Requests for confirmed adults still use restricted age treatment and a G content-rating cap. This mixed-audience Android configuration removes advertising-ID and ad-services advertising-ID, attribution and topics permissions.

Deleting local information

You can remove local game information, including any stored age-eligibility category, through Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the game. Device or platform backups, if enabled, are controlled by the platform provider. DAAR GAMES cannot remotely access or delete game progress stored only on your device.

Support enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and investigate the issue, and retain it as needed to handle the enquiry and any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Google Play and this website

Google Play handles store, installation and account information under Google's policies. This website has no contact form, advertising or analytics scripts added by DAAR GAMES. Its hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and secure the website.
